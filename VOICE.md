# Voice

The house rules for my writing. The linter in `scripts/chrisnizer_lint.py` enforces
the mechanical parts. The judgment parts are here, for me or an agent to apply by
hand. These rules were distilled from my own solo writing, the AI-writing tells a
humanizer catches, and the register of my research papers. The actual writing
samples they came from stay in `VOICE.local.md`, which is untracked.

## Who is writing

I build playful, embodied human-augmentation systems: e-textiles, electrical
muscle stimulation games, wearables, WaterHCI. I write in three modes. Academic
prose for papers, where "we" and a contribution frame are correct. Team and
stakeholder documents, which use a neutral voice with no "I", "my", "we" or
"our", so they read the same whoever wrote them. Direct prose for everything
else, which is what most of these rules are about.

## Reader first

Before the rules, name who reads the piece and what they do with it. Cut what that
reader doesn't need before rewording anything. In someone else's document, propose
the cut and let the owner decide. The piece is done when someone who
has seen none of the earlier work reads it once without stopping. The rules in this
file serve that test. When a rule makes a sentence longer or stranger, the sentence
wins.

## Structure

Lead with the point. Do not bury the lede. In a message or an email, say who I am
and what I want in the first two sentences. In a document, open with what it says
or decides.

Write flowing paragraphs when the content is narrative. Fold labelled fragments
into prose. Reach for a list only when the content is a list, and a table when
the items share the same attributes, such as options that each have a cost and
downsides.

Name what you count. "The three", "the rest" and "a few" leave the reader asking
which ones, so list them or give the number. Point at things by name, not by
place: "Section 04" or "teams and managers", not "below" or "above the person".

Use one name for one thing. If the page calls it a workflow, it is a workflow
everywhere, and a pronoun that opens a paragraph gets replaced by the name.

Describe what is true now. A document doesn't recount its own earlier versions.
A number with nothing behind it reads "not identified yet", and an estimate is
called an estimate each time it appears.

One idea per sentence. My first drafts run to dense compound sentences that stack
three clauses with "and". Catch those and split them.

## Tone

Professional and personable. Not stiff, not overly casual. A message or an email
gets a friendly opener, a direct ask and a plain sign-off. A document has no
opener and no sign-off.

No boosterism. Nothing is groundbreaking, seamless, or best-in-class, and no
effort is a big lift.

Confident and matter-of-fact. State what I want plainly. Do not hedge.

## Mechanics

No em dashes. This is my preference, not a sign of anything. Use a comma, a period,
a colon or parentheses, or restructure the sentence, and vary which one.

Active voice. "The model reads the gaze path" beats "the gaze path is read by the
model." Name the actor.

Minimal filler. I cut it from AI drafts, so I avoid it in my own.

First person singular for solo work. "I" and "my", not "we" and "our", unless it
is a co-authored paper.

## Calibration

These rules are inferred from a handful of samples. The rhythm and sentence-length
rules in particular sharpen with a longer unedited sample. Those samples live in
`samples/` and `VOICE.local.md`, both untracked; revise these rules against them.
