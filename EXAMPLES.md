# Examples

Real before-and-after pairs, to calibrate a rewrite against. Each "before" passed
the linter clean and still read oddly, which is why the stranger test exists.
They come from the README and on-screen text of
[Micro Garden](https://github.com/ChristianNHill/micro-garden), rewritten on
2026-09-21.

## Stilted or literary

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| A lawn sits in a bowl of rock, with a pond under a waterfall. | It is a lawn surrounded by rock walls, with a pond under a waterfall. |
| `F` shakes fruit down from the tree, and so does a tap on it. | Press `F` or click the tree to shake fruit down. |
| A halftone screen of ink shades everything, and night is a wash of blue. | The shading uses a halftone dot pattern, and the night is tinted blue. |
| A duck sits or stands about until boredom sets it wandering. | A duck sits or stands around until it gets bored and wanders off. |
| Click the box to step its volume, and off means off for the ducks too. | Click the box to change its volume. When you turn it off, the ducks stop hearing it too. |
| Friendships and trust are slow quantities that events push about. | Friendships and trust are values that change slowly as things happen. |
| Personalities are presets over continuous dials, in the style of the Chao. | Personalities are preset combinations of continuous traits, like the Chao's. |

## A rule written as a riddle

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| The tree stops dropping fruit by itself once four lie on the lawn, and your shakes keep working up to twelve. | The tree also drops fruit on its own until there are four pieces on the lawn, and shaking it works until there are twelve. |
| Two other results stand because they are true. | Two other results look like problems, but I report them as they are. |

## Private jargon

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| There are two bodies. One is a 2D body in Python. | The ducks can be simulated in two ways: a simple 2D simulation in Python, or the robot's own physics simulation. |
| You see its two hex retinas and what its descending neurons ask of its legs. | An overlay shows what its two compound eyes see and how strongly its descending neurons are telling its legs to move. |
| `O` hides its eyes | `O` hides what it sees |

## Too clipped

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| The walking policy upstream ships does not yet track a velocity. | The walking policy that ships upstream does not yet track a commanded velocity. |
| tap the grass: let it go | tap the grass: deselect it |
| needs: hunger, thirst, sleep, tired, bored | needs: hungry, thirsty, sleepy, tired, bored |
