# Examples

Real before-and-after pairs, to calibrate a rewrite against. Each "before" passed
the linter clean and still read oddly, which is why the stranger test exists.
They come from the README and on-screen text of
[Micro Garden](https://github.com/ChristianNHill/micro-garden), rewritten on
2026-09-21.

## Stilted or literary

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| A lawn sits in a bowl of rock, with a pond under a waterfall. | It is a lawn surrounded by rock walls, with a pond under a waterfall. |
| `F` shakes fruit down from the tree, and so does a tap on it. | Press `F` or click the tree to shake fruit down. |
| A halftone screen of ink shades everything, and night is a wash of blue. | The shading uses a halftone dot pattern, and the night is tinted blue. |
| A duck sits or stands about until boredom sets it wandering. | A duck sits or stands around until it gets bored and wanders off. |
| Click the box to step its volume, and off means off for the ducks too. | Click the box to change its volume. When you turn it off, the ducks stop hearing it too. |
| Friendships and trust are slow quantities that events push about. | Friendships and trust are values that change slowly as things happen. |
| Personalities are presets over continuous dials, in the style of the Chao. | Personalities are preset combinations of continuous traits, like the Chao's. |

## A rule written as a riddle

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| The tree stops dropping fruit by itself once four lie on the lawn, and your shakes keep working up to twelve. | The tree also drops fruit on its own until there are four pieces on the lawn, and shaking it works until there are twelve. |
| Two other results stand because they are true. | Two other results look like problems, but I report them as they are. |

## Private jargon

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| There are two bodies. One is a 2D body in Python. | The ducks can be simulated in two ways: a simple 2D simulation in Python, or the robot's own physics simulation. |
| You see its two hex retinas and what its descending neurons ask of its legs. | An overlay shows what its two compound eyes see and how strongly its descending neurons are telling its legs to move. |
| `O` hides its eyes | `O` hides what it sees |

## Too clipped

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| The walking policy upstream ships does not yet track a velocity. | The walking policy that ships upstream does not yet track a commanded velocity. |
| tap the grass: let it go | tap the grass: deselect it |
| needs: hunger, thirst, sleep, tired, bored | needs: hungry, thirsty, sleepy, tired, bored |

## Team documents

Made-up pairs in the shape of edits to a stakeholder document, 2026-11-03. Each
"before" reads fine on its own, and the reader sent each one back.

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| The missing ones include the three the launch needs most. | Three of the missing pieces block the launch: a sign-in, an export and a delete button. |
| Two settings have no default. | The timeout and the retry limit have no default. |
| The parser rarely handles dates. | The parser handled 3 of 40 date formats. |
| Reports for people above the user are planned. | Reports for team leads and managers are planned. |
| Later stages hide the error. | The summary step hides the error. |
| Hosting and support are the costs nobody has measured. | Hosting isn't priced yet. Support is estimated at one day a week. |
| Reviewed by legal before launch. | Legal reviews it before launch. |
| It works with 12 web addresses. | It works with 3 providers: Mail, Calendar and Drive. |
| The research team owns the threshold. | The project team sets the threshold. |

## Team documents, second round

Made-up pairs in the shape of edits to a long stakeholder page, 2026-11-19. The
page had passed the linter on every run before a reviewer raised these. The pairs
that drop a figure or reopen a decision are content changes, which the owner
approved.

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| The following table lists the options. (The same opener on twelve tables.) | Each row is one option, with what it needs and its downside: |
| Earlier versions of this page gave 3 cents per call. The page now uses the larger test. | A call costs 4 cents, measured on the larger test. |
| The build takes roughly 10 to 20 weeks. | The build time is not identified yet. |
| The target is to keep summaries on the device. | The target is not decided yet. One option being explored is keeping summaries on the device. |
| On `main` at `a1b2c3d` that alert can't fire. | Since 4 March, an alert fires only if its rule lists a matching event. This alert lists none. So on the current `main` code (`a1b2c3d`, 4 March), it can't fire. |
| The export row has no figure. (Two paragraphs on, a cost for the export.) | The export costs 0.2 cents per call. The five test runs made no exports, so the figure comes from a separate run. |
| Cost per person per day is the cost per call times the calls that person makes in a day. | Cost per person per day = cost per call × calls per person per day. The page has the cost per call. The calls per day aren't measured yet. |
| The "matched" check runs after each call. | The "matched" check runs after each call. It reads the suggestion and the next request, and records whether the request followed the suggestion. |
| Because the server keeps no log, the proxy's log is the only source. | The proxy's log is the only source, because the server keeps no log. |
| Cheaper option | Optimization |
